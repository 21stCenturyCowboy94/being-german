In Germany, there are many companies that serve worldwide, especially in ther USA.


German companies sell a number of goods to the US, including Automobiles, electronics, medical technology, and even candy.


In terms of technology, One company in Germany that sells a number of items to the US is Siemens. Siemens sells trains, medical machines, electronics, and other machinery to the US.


Automobiles are Germany's most well known product, with Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, and Audi being popular brands.


Even candy is sold by a famous German company. This company is called Haribo, and it sells gummy candies to the rest of the world.
